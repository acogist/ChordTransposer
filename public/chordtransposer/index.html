How to use

  1. Open a file – Tap Open and choose a chord chart (text, PDF, or image). Text files and text-based PDFs work best. Text from images or scanned PDFs may not be read correctly.
  2. Or enter chords – Paste or type a chord chart in the Input area. Output updates as you edit.
  3. Transpose – Choose the key shift. Chords in Output are converted.
  4. Accidental – Switch between # and b.
  5. Size – Change the text size.
  6. Preview – See the whole result at once.
  7. Save – Save the result.
